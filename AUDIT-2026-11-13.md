# Audit, 13 Nov 2026 (AutoTune 2.30.1)

The app, its UI and UX, the process flow, and the link between the phone and AutoTune on a TV or a Windows PC.

How it was done: from the code, the docs, the Windows window renders (`./gradlew :desktop:shots`) and the store screenshots. There was no Android SDK in that session, so the phone and TV screens were not rendered or run. `:dsp`, `:link` and `:desktop` tests passed (89, 6 and 22).

The TV and the Windows app never talk to each other. Each is a server the phone connects to, one at a time, so "the connection" means phone to TV and phone to PC.

Status: every item was open when this was written. Mark an item "done in X.Y.Z" when it is fixed.

Update, 13 Nov 2026: worked through in 2.31.0. The Android changes were not compiled or run (no Android SDK in that session either); `:dsp`, `:link` and `:desktop` tests pass (89, 12 and 26).

## Connection: phone to TV or PC

Ordered by how much each would hurt in use.

1. **Stop on the phone does not stop the TV or PC.** `stop()` only cancels the phone's job (`TheatreViewModel.stop`). Only the level test sends `quiet`, so a stopped distances, EQ or EQ-check test keeps playing its sweeps to the end, with the EQ held off. **Done in 2.31.0:** `stop()` sends `quiet` whenever a test was running; the TV (`stopPlaying`) and PC (`playJob.cancel`) stop a pass or clip on it and release the EQ hold (`PcEndpointTest.quietStopsAPassAndTheEqComesBack`).
2. **A dropped link mid-test is reported as a volume problem.** Tests never check that the link is still open. A pass ends with "The start beeps were not heard: turn the volume up" (`measurePassAsIs`), and the level test listens to silence for up to 180 s per speaker (`levelTest`). **Done in 2.31.0:** a link that closes while a test runs ends it at once (`lostMidTest`), the level test included, with "The connection to the TV/PC was lost"; nothing is saved.
3. **A dead link is noticed slowly, or never.** The phone's ping gets no answer and neither side has a read timeout (`LinkServer.serve` sets `soTimeout = 0` after the hello). If the TV loses power the phone can show "connected" for minutes. The TV shows a vanished phone as connected until another pairs, which also blocks Pretend (`TvViewModel.setPretend`). **Done in 2.31.0:** the TV or PC answers `ping` with `pong` (announced as `pong` in the welcome caps); the phone reads with a 30 s timeout from such a companion; the hello carries `ping=10` and the server drops a phone silent for three periods (`LinkServerTest`). Old phones, TVs and PCs keep the old behaviour.
4. **The phone's screen can switch off while measuring.** Only the TV screen sets keep-screen-on (`TvActivity`). A multi-seat EQ test, the level test or a 5-spot car reading can run past the screen timeout. **Done in 2.31.0:** `KeepScreenOn` on the theatre page while busy and on the measuring screen while measuring or between spots (not run on a phone).
5. **The companion keeps playing when the phone goes.** Level noise loops for up to two minutes; nothing stops on disconnect (`TvViewModel` and `PcEndpoint`, `onPhone`). **Done in 2.31.0:** `onPhone(null)` stops what plays and releases the EQ hold on the TV and PC (`PcEndpointTest.aPhoneThatGoesStopsWhatItStarted`).
6. **The pairing code is weak.** Four digits, never changed. The 1.5 s pause applies per connection and connections are unlimited, so anyone on the Wi-Fi can try all 10,000 in seconds (`LinkServer.serve`). The damage is bounded: test sounds and an EQ clamped to +6 dB. **Done in 2.31.0:** one global gate for wrong codes, a pause doubling after three wrong in a row (up to 60 s), at most 8 waiting unpaired connections, hello lines over 1 KB refused. Still four digits; remembered pairings unaffected (`LinkServerTest`).
7. **The address and announcement are set once at start.** If the PC or TV starts before the network is up, or its address changes, the phone never finds it (`desktop Main.kt`, `TvViewModel` init). A failed TV registration is ignored (`announce`). **Done in 2.31.0:** the PC checks its addresses every 15 s and re-announces on a change; the TV uses a default-network callback to refresh the address and re-register, and retries a failed registration up to five times (the TV part not run on a device).
8. **Connect by address assumes port 47810** (`TheatreViewModel.connectAddress`). The server falls back to any free port when that one is taken, and the PC window shows the address without the port. **Done in 2.31.0:** `parseAddress` takes host or host:port ([v6]:port); the TV and PC show the port when it is not 47810.
9. **Reconnects can collide.** The page, the retry loop and the widget all call `CompanionLink.ensure()` with no lock, and every `LinkClient.connect` starts by closing the current link. **Done in 2.31.0:** one `Mutex` around `CompanionLink.connect` and `ensure`.
10. **The TV is reachable only while its screen is open.** The server lives in the TV screen's view model, so the phone widget cannot toggle the EQ once the TV app is closed. The PC stays reachable from the tray. **Still open:** moving `LinkServer` into `TvEqService` changes the TV's lifecycle (playback and the screen state live in `TvViewModel`) and could not be tried without a TV, so it was left. In 2.31.0 the widget says it plainly instead ("Open AutoTune on the TV"; the toast: "AutoTune must be open on the TV").
11. **"Sent" is not confirmed.** `useEq()` logs and saves the EQ as sent even when the link is closed (`LinkClient.send` drops it silently); nothing tells the user. A stale EQ error is also never cleared by a later good `eqstate`. **Done in 2.31.0:** EQ on and off try `ensure()` when the link is closed, then show one line ("The TV is not connected: the EQ was not sent"); the widget reports a failed send; a good `eqstate` clears the EQ error an earlier one left.
12. **Windows firewall is unverified.** HANDOVER already lists it. The code adds no firewall rule, so a refused prompt most likely leaves the PC unreachable. **Partly, in 2.31.0:** the one-time administrator step (Allow AutoTune) now adds an inbound rule for AutoTune.exe on private networks (by TCP port when the program is not known; it never fails the step). Still unverified on Windows, and a PC that never runs that step relies on Windows' own prompt.

The link module has six tests, none for drops, timeouts or stopping. **Done in 2.31.0:** twelve now, with pong, silence, throttling, the waiting cap and host:port; the PC adds quiet and disconnect tests.

## What gets in a first-time user's way most

1. **A test with no names is called by its date.** Both name fields can be left empty. The test is then "13 Nov 20:26" on the Tune screen ("Continue “13 Nov 20:26”"), on Tests, as the results page's big title, and as the EQ's name, where it sits over a second date ("1.3 dB from target · 13 Nov 20:28"). In Refine and Result the title is the full auto name, "Car, Harman-style (+8 dB bass) test, 13 No…", cut off, with the target name the rules say not to repeat. Simplest: name it after the equipment picked ("Car", then "Car 2") when nothing is typed. **Done in 2.32.0:** named "Car", "Car 2", "Home theatre", "PC speakers", "Speakers" (or the equipment name typed), never a date or the target; saved tests keep their names; Refine and Result titles go through `shortTestName`.
2. **On the Tune screen the big red key starts a new test while an unfinished one waits** in a small grey "Continue" under it. With a test open, Continue should be the main key and "new test" the small one. **Done in 2.32.0:** Continue is the dial key, New test under it.
3. **After Keep, nothing says it is finished.** The Tune screen shows the Tuned card, "AutoTune EQ is on with it." and still offers Continue. Tests' ⋮ menu also still says "Continue tuning" for a finished test, while its results page says "Tune further". **Done in 2.32.0:** a finished tune shows the Tuned card and no Continue; the ⋮ menu says "Tune further" for a finished test.
4. **After the app is closed and opened again, Tune shows neither the Tuned card nor Continue**; the test has to be found on Tests, and its "open now" dot is gone. **Done in 2.32.0:** the open test, its tuned card and its resume step are kept in pref `openTest` and reopened on start (checked with a restart in the harness).
5. **"Nothing found yet" is a dead end in Simple.** The address field is Advanced only, and the code field shows before anything is found. Offer the address (or what to check) once nothing is found after a few seconds. **Done in 2.32.0:** the address field appears in Simple after 6 s with nothing found.
6. **Back from the Connect page goes to "What are you tuning?"**, not to the names screen it came from; the answers and typed names are lost. The page's title is "Home theatre" for a PC too, and it is cut to "Home th…" by the mode key. **Done in 2.32.0:** Back returns to the Set up screen it came from with its answers (`SetupDraft`); the title is "PC speakers" or "Home theatre"; the slim title is drawn smaller rather than cut.
7. **Measure the stock system needs two presses the first time.** The first press asks for the microphone; after Allow nothing starts. It should measure as soon as access is given. While access is missing, the screen has two dark keys (Allow microphone, Measure the stock system) and a green dot beside "Phone microphone" over an amber "Microphone access needed". **Done in 2.32.0:** the key reads "Allow the mic and measure" and measures once access is given; one amber line, one key.
8. **Three mic spots are never announced in Simple**, and each needs a press. The first hint is "spot 1 of 3" on the measuring screen. (Starting the next spot by itself is already in `BACKLOG.md`.) **Done in 2.32.0:** Set up says "3 mic spots · each starts by itself"; the next spot starts after a 5 s countdown with Pause (BACKLOG item cleared).

2. Start measuring as soon as the microphone is allowed. **Done in 2.32.0.**
3. Start the next mic spot by itself after a short countdown (already in the backlog): two presses fewer per reading, four per tune. **Done in 2.32.0.**
4. Make Continue the main key on Tune while a test is unfinished. **Done in 2.32.0.**
5. On Refine, put the three ways to apply in one list with the recommended one first, each with one line saying what it does. **Done in 2.32.0.**
6. End a tune with a clear finished state on Tune (the Tuned card, the EQ on, no Continue), and keep it after the app restarts. **Done in 2.32.0.**
